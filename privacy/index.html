privacy

Last updated: 2026-05-06.

what we collect

  • Submissions. The photo or video you upload, plus any caption and display name you choose.
  • Anti-fraud hash. When you submit, vote, or report, we compute a SHA-256 hash from your IP address + user-agent + a salt, and store the hash. We do not store your raw IP address.
  • Server logs. Cloudflare records short-term request logs (IP, user-agent, URL) for security and anti-abuse purposes. We don't keep our own copy.

what we don't collect

No accounts. No email. No passwords. No tracking cookies. No third-party advertising. No analytics that follow you between sites.

how we use it

  • To run the site (host your submission, count votes, show leaderboards).
  • To prevent vote manipulation and submission spam.
  • To respond to abuse and copyright complaints.

retention

Approved submissions are kept indefinitely while the site exists. Rejected/removed submissions are kept for 30 days for dispute purposes, then deleted. Anti-fraud hashes age out as votes/reports age out.

your rights

To delete a submission you posted, email hi@cowboysdocowboyshit.com with the permalink. We will remove it within a reasonable timeframe.
