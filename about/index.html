about

Cowboys do cowboy shit. The internet decides how cowboy. That's the whole site.

You upload a photo or short clip. We put it in a queue. We approve it (or we don't, see why-removed). It goes in the feed. Strangers vote it up or down. The leaderboard ranks the most-cowboy stuff today, this week, all time.

rules

  • It has to be yours, or you have permission to post it.
  • No nudity. No gore. No animals being hurt for the bit.
  • If a person is in it and it's identifying, they're cool with being on the internet.
  • 18+.
